02 CORE ETHOS & APPROACH

ORDER THROUGH RESTRAINT.

RADICAL MINIMALISM AS A FUNCTIONAL WEAPON

在视觉信息过载的时代，最强大的设计并非堆砌更多感官刺激，而是精准剥离一切非本质的装饰。我的创作根植于纯粹的结构主义：通过极度考究的栅格排版、冷冽克制的色彩秩序与锋利的负空间，赋予每个数字载体不可动摇的建筑级重量。

Every pixel, transition, and micro-interaction must earn its existence. I collaborate closely with forward-thinking tech pioneers, luxury maisons, and cultural institutions to translate complex technological and artistic visions into effortless, timeless digital reality.

// 01. ZERO COMPROMISE

CHISELED GEOMETRY

No ornamental curves without structural justification. Sharp angles focus pure intent.

// 02. KINETIC POISE

INTENTIONAL MOTION

Dynamic states driven by physics and cursor interaction rather than superficial decoration.
